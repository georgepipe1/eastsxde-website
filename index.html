Press kit

For promoters and press

EASTSXDE are Jack Self and George Pipe, a DJ and producer duo working between Nottingham and Suffolk. They make UK garage and house built on bouncy drums and groovy basslines.

They have been releasing since 2023: the two-track Soul Music, a run of singles, the four-track Avora EP in 2025, then Land Of The Lost and Toujours in 2026.

Privacy notice

What this site collects. Nothing from you directly. There are no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms, and nothing is stored on your device. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the act, or taking steps towards a contract with you. We keep those emails only as long as we need them for that and for our records.
